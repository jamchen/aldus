# 0.2.0-next.18

A prerelease on the `next` dist-tag. **Not promoted to `latest`** — promotion is the owner's
decision (ADR-0023).

Both halves of the #133 ruling: a take now records three facts instead of one, and paidness is
derived from evidence that money was actually spent.

## A take records what was requested, what produced the bytes, and how they were delivered

`SynthesisGateway` built a take from the plan — `text` and `parameters` assigned before the adapter
ran — while their documentation read _"as actually sent"_ and _"as actually used"_. `SynthesisOutcome`
had no field for either, so an adapter that was not the planned provider had no way to say so.

Two instances arrived from one adopter, in one adapter:

- seven takes synthesised by a local engine recorded the planned hosted provider, so _"which takes
  were paid for"_ answered from `parameters.provider` returns seven charges that never happened;
- their plan carries 36 performance tags their local engine speaks aloud rather than reads, so the
  adapter strips them and the take recorded text the engine never received.

The second is the sharper one. `finalProviderText` documented itself as the string §13.2 binds
against the operator's approval, and was assigned _from_ the approved plan. Nothing consumed it, so
nothing was broken in a running system. What was broken is that the comparison it exists for could
not fire once anyone wrote it: both sides are the same expression, so the check would pass and mean
nothing.

**Delivery is a third fact, not a refinement of the second.** A replay adapter delivers audio
another provider produced: its produced facts are honestly that provider's, and its delivery called
nobody and cost nothing. `TakeRecord.delivery` carries `adapterId`, an open-string `mechanism`,
`incurredCharge`, and provenance for replay and import.

`adapterId` is supplied by the gateway, never taken from the outcome — a component that can state
its own identity can state a false one.

## Paidness comes from charge evidence

```ts
// before
return take.authorization !== undefined || take.costRecordId !== undefined;
```

An authorization means spending was **permitted**, not that it **occurred**. The adopter's seven
authorized local renders were all counted paid — the same shape as `parameters.provider`, in the
same records, for the third time.

`takePaidness` is three-valued: `paid`, `free`, `unknown`. **`unknown` is not `free`** — absence of
a cost record does not establish that nothing was charged. `isPaid` is retained and narrowed to
`paid` only.

`SynthesisOutcome.charged` is finally read. It was documented as _"what it cost, where the adapter
knows before a cost record exists"_ and nothing consumed it; the composed stack's own fake adapter
had been reporting an amount since it was written.

## Paid divergence is refused before the provider call

A paid execution whose text or parameters differ from those the authorization bound is refused
before the provider is called, or needs a newly authorized plan. A free local render or replay may
diverge, and its record exposes the divergence.

_Before the call_ is only reachable through a declaration — a divergence is visible after the call,
when a paid provider has already been billed. An adapter declaring `maySubstitute` is refused
pre-flight when the segment is expected to be paid. One that does not declare and diverges anyway is
caught after and recorded through the unauthorized-charge path: the money is gone, and a charge that
appears nowhere is a larger harm than an ugly record.

## New public API

From `@aldus-runtime/tts-ledger`:

| symbol                                      | answers                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `producedParameters(take)`                  | what made these bytes — `undefined` means unknown, never "same as requested" |
| `producedFinalProviderText(take)`           | the string actually sent                                                     |
| `compareProducedToRequested(take)`          | `unknown` \| `matches` \| `diverged`                                         |
| `takePaidness(take)`                        | `paid` \| `free` \| `unknown`                                                |
| `takeDeliverySchema`, `producedFactsSchema` | the record shapes                                                            |

From `@aldus-runtime/stage-runner` (shipped in `next.17`, repeated here because it is new to anyone
upgrading from `next.15`): `EvaluationFinding`, and `StageOutcome`'s `evaluated` arm.

## Migration

None required. Existing records keep their bytes and their meaning: missing facts decode as
**unknown**, never as "matched" and never as "free". An adapter that reports nothing behaves exactly
as it did.

`SCHEMA_VERSION` 1.6 → 1.7 (additive optional fields, MINOR per ADR-0003).

## Known limitations

- An adapter that never learned to report production facts is indistinguishable from one with
  nothing to report. No function over a take can tell them apart; the limit is documented on the
  fields rather than left to be discovered.
- `latest` still points at `0.1.0`.

ADR-0037, ADR-0038, ADR-0039. Contract §12 and §15 amended in both language editions.
